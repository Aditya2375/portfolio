Instagram Growth Bot

Follower-growth automation linked to my Instagram account. It walks the Discover People feed and scores every account it surfaces — mutual count, follow-back ratio and other signals consolidated into a single score. Over a set threshold, it sends a follow request, waits a set window, and unfollows accounts that don't follow back. Hit Instagram's rate limits; experiment concluded.
